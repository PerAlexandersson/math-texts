\section[availableTexts]{Available texts}

This site collects mathematical texts that are developed independently but
share one typesetting, bibliography, navigation, and search system.

\begin{itemize}
\item \href{colored-matroids/index.htm}{Colored matroids and symmetric cycle
exchange}: an evolving research text developed alongside a Lean
formalization.
\item \textbf{Symmetric functions}: course notes, planned under
\texttt{/symfun/}.
\item \textbf{Discrete mathematics}: lecture notes for MM2001, planned under
\texttt{/mm2001dm/}.
\end{itemize}

The latter two projects are placeholders and have not yet been published.
