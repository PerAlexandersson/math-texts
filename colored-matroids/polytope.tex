\metatitle{Assignment polytopes}
\metadescription{Assignment-vector geometry and open polyhedral questions for colored matroids.}

\section[polytope]{The assignment-vector embedding}

A coloring can be represented by its zero-one vector in the coordinates
$E\times C$. The convex hull of the vectors belonging to a fixed-quota
family is its \defin{assignment polytope}. Fixed row and color sums supply
immediate affine equations; the exchange axiom imposes additional geometry.

\section[polytopeEdges]{Edges and cycle moves}

Simple color-cycle switches give sparse directions with entries in
$\{-1,0,1\}$. This chapter will separate the proved implication from
symmetric cycle exchange to simple-cycle edge directions from the open
converse for arbitrary nonempty fixed-quota families.

\section[polytopeDescriptions]{Inequality descriptions}

The principal question is whether every assignment polytope arising from
symmetric cycle exchange admits a complete description, including its affine
hull, with coefficients in $\{-1,0,1\}$. The site will record established
classes, reductions, counterexamples to stronger formulations, and the exact
remaining obligations.

\begin{remark}
The unrestricted coefficient description and the unrestricted edge converse
are open. They must not be used as dependencies of proved statements.
\end{remark}
