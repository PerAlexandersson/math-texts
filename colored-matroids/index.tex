\section[mainProblems]{Main problems}

We collect seven open problems below. Each heading has a permanent link;
the chapter links give definitions and established constructions.

\subsection[problem1]{1. Lorentzian color projections}

Let $\mathcal F$ be a colored matroid on positions $E$, fix a color $c$,
and put $B(u,c)=\{e:u(e)=c\}$. For positive cell weights $w_{e,d}$, consider
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 Q_{\mathcal F,c}(\mathbf x;\mathbf w)\\
 =\sum_{u\in\mathcal F}
   \left(\prod_{e:u(e)\ne c}w_{e,u(e)}\right)
   \prod_{e\in B(u,c)}x_e.
 \end{gathered}
\]
Is this polynomial Lorentzian for every choice of the weights?
Each full word contributes separately, so the coefficients retain all
hidden completions. The target quota-two case asks that the corresponding
quadratic Hessian have at most one positive eigenvalue.
See \href{rank.htm#lorentzianConjecture}{completion polynomials} and
\href{reductions.htm}{the reductions}.

\subsection[problem2]{2. Realizing $P$-Eulerian polynomials}

For a naturally labeled poset $P$, write
$W_P(t)=\sum_{\pi\in\mathcal L(P)}t^{\operatorname{des}(\pi)}$, where
$\mathcal L(P)$ is its set of linear extensions. Which posets admit a
colored matroid $\mathcal F$, a color $c$, marked positions $S$, and a
bijection $\pi\mapsto u_\pi$ onto $\mathcal F$ such that
\[
 \operatorname{des}(\pi)=|B(u_\pi,c)\cap S|?
\]
More generally, can we use a word family whose selected-color projection
is Lorentzian at every positive hidden cell weighting, which we call the
selected-color PIL property? Determine the attainable target quota and
preserve the full coefficients under the specialization $x_e=t$ on $S$
and $x_e=1$ elsewhere. The
\href{series-parallel.htm#seriesParallelLift}{series-parallel construction}
and \href{log-concavity.htm}{width-three models} provide starting cases.

\subsection[problem3]{3. Describing assignment polytopes}

Does every colored-matroid assignment polytope have a complete description
by affine equations and inequalities with coefficients in $\{-1,0,1\}$?
The coordinates are the original position--color cells, and a complete
description must include the affine hull as well as the relative facets.
See \href{polytope.htm#generalPolytopeDescription}{the coefficient question};
\href{projections.htm}{retaining only some color columns} gives a different
problem, for which integer coefficient sizes are unbounded.

\subsection[problem4]{4. Equivalence of the exchange axioms}

Suppose a fixed-quota word family satisfies prescribed one-sided cycle
exchange: for every two words and every prescribed disagreement position,
some simple color cycle through that position gives a feasible first
offspring. Must it satisfy symmetric cycle exchange, with a choice of cycle
for which both offspring are feasible?
See \href{intro.htm#cyclicExchange}{the two exchange axioms} and the related
\href{polytope.htm#generalPolytopeDescription}{cycle-edge characterization}.

\subsection[problem5]{5. Intrinsic rank axioms}

For a colored matroid with colors $1,\dotsc,m$ and sets $X_c\subseteq E$,
its joint rank is
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 R(X_1,\dotsc,X_m)\\
 =\max_{u\in\mathcal F}\sum_{c=1}^m|B(u,c)\cap X_c|.
 \end{gathered}
\]
Which intrinsic axioms characterize the functions arising this way?
In particular, which compatibility relations between different color
arguments supplement the ordinary matroid rank properties of the individual
slices? See \href{rank-flats.htm#jointRank}{joint rank} and the
\href{rank-flats.htm#valuatedCompletions}{rank-axiom question}.

\subsection[problem6]{6. Multiple symmetric exchange}

Take two words $u,v$ of a colored matroid, a color $c$, and a prescribed
subset $I\subseteq B(u,c)\setminus B(v,c)$. Must there be a set
$J\subseteq B(v,c)\setminus B(u,c)$ with $|J|=|I|$ and two feasible words
$u',v'$ such that
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 B(u',c)=(B(u,c)\setminus I)\cup J,\\
 B(v',c)=(B(v,c)\setminus J)\cup I,
 \end{gathered}
\]
and the unordered pair of letters at each position is unchanged?
The requirement concerns compatible full words, beyond multiple basis
exchange in the projection matroid. A first case to address has four
positions in $B(u,c)\setminus B(v,c)$ and two in $I$.
See \href{exchange-graphs.htm}{cycle exchanges} and
\href{rank-flats.htm#valuatedCompletions}{best completion scores}.

\subsection[problem7]{7. Bruhat intervals and cycle closure}

Let $[u,v]$ be an interval in the strong Bruhat order on permutations.
Its support board consists of the cells $(i,\sigma(i))$ occurring in at
least one $\sigma\in[u,v]$. Write $\mathcal R$ for all permutations
supported on this board. Is $[u,v]$ a colored matroid exactly when
$[u,v]=\mathcal R$? More strongly, does its closure under individual
relative-cycle exchanges always equal $\mathcal R$?
A related recognition problem asks which boards occur as supports of
Bruhat intervals. See
\href{permutations.htm#relativeCycles}{relative-cycle closure} for the
quota-one exchange condition.
