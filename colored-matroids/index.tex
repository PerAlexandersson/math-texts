\section[chapters]{Chapters}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \href{intro.htm}{\textbf{Introduction}}. Words, ordered set partitions,
and symmetric cycle exchange.

\item \href{operations.htm}{\textbf{Operations and minors}}. Color symmetry,
loops and coloops, restrictions, score maximizers, color-indexed minors, and
direct sums.

\item \href{exchange-graphs.htm}{\textbf{Exchange graphs}}. Monotone exchange
paths, ordinary basis graphs, and decomposition into direct-sum components.

\item \href{independence.htm}{\textbf{Independence and circuits}}. Partial
assignments, ordinary matroid independence in two colors, and mixed circuits.

\item \href{rank-flats.htm}{\textbf{Rank functions and flats}}. Joint rank,
ordinary ranks in each color, conditional flats, common contractions,
and best completion scores.

\item \href{graphic.htm}{\textbf{Graphs and colored matroids}}. Graphic
matroids, fixed-indegree orientations, directed-cycle exchange, and cuts.

\item \href{constructions.htm}{\textbf{Constructions and examples}}. Allowed
colors, matchings and weighted assignments, interval lattice paths, and
representable lifts.

\item \href{lattice-paths.htm}{\textbf{Colored lattice-path matroids}}.
Prefix-count bounds, path polytopes, and regions in the quota box.

\item \href{tutte.htm}{\textbf{Distance and Tutte polynomials}}. A distance
enumerator, its tropical minor recurrence, and the classical Tutte polynomial
in two colors.

\item \href{log-concavity.htm}{\textbf{Ultra-log-concavity}}. Rigid
width-three posets and encodings by disjoint matroid bases.

\item \href{series-parallel.htm}{\textbf{Series-parallel posets and stable lifts}}.
Linear extensions, a recursive colored-matroid construction, and real-stable
color marginals.

\item \href{rank.htm}{\textbf{Color projections and M-convexity}}. Count
vectors, their rank description, and the Lorentzian conjecture for completion
polynomials.

\item \href{reductions.htm}{\textbf{Reductions of the Lorentzian conjecture}}.
Binary encodings, weights as completion counts, a rank-two counting
equivalence, and reduction with a fixed number of colors.

\item \href{polytope.htm}{\textbf{Assignment polytopes}}. Assignment-vector
geometry, faces and cycle edges, lattice-path inequalities, flow optimization,
and integer decomposition.
\end{enumerate}
